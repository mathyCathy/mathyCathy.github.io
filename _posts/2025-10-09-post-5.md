Someone recently proposed using Bland-Altman plots to look at agreement between two ordinal scales taking on integer values from 0 to 5. This intuitively felt wrong, because I recalled that Bland-Altman limits of agreement depended on a normality assumption. I decided to think about this a little bit more.
